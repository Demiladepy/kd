# KudiDesk Desk Policy

## Fees & Pricing
- **Service fee:** 1.5% of invoice amount
- **Quote validity:** 10 minutes (locked rate)
- **Rate:** Set daily by desk operator (NGN per USD)
- **Minimum payment:** $5 USD
- **Per-customer limits:** KD-001 max $50, KD-002 max $30

## Settlement
- **Payment method:** Naira via Paystack (test mode)
- **Payout:** Dollar stablecoin (USDC) on Base Sepolia testnet
- **Settlement time:** Usually minutes after payment verification
- **What "on-chain" means:** The USDC transfer is recorded on the Base Sepolia blockchain - you can verify it on Basescan

## Treasury
- Treasury maintains USDC balance for settlements
- If treasury is low, payments are paused pending top-up by human operator

## Support Hours
- **Human support:** Business hours (Lagos time)
- For urgent issues outside quotes/payments flow, escalate to a human
